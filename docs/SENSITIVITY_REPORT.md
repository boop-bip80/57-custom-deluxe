Reading the tables: H2 and H3 at a fixed low drive sit 40-60 dB below the fundamental, so a small change of bias moves them by many dB; they rank high for almost every electrical parameter and say 'the harmonic balance needs the real operating point', not 'this component is dominant'. The column 'features outside the Sec. 26 tolerance' is therefore the better guide to what a measurement must pin down. `speaker_Mms` shows no electrical effect on purpose: with fs, Qms and Qes held fixed the electrical equivalent circuit (R_es = Re Qms / Qes, C_mes = Qes / (w0 Re), L_ces = Re / (w0 Qes)) does not depend on Mms; Mms only sets the acoustic sensitivity (cone velocity per volt), which lies outside the electrical boundary.
